# Pinecil V2 Travel Case

A compact travel case for the Pinecil V2: the iron (with a tip fitted), four
spare tips and a small hex key, each in its own shaped channel, so nothing
rattles. Magnets click it shut, and a loop of velcro holds it closed. The
PINE64 pinecone is cut through the cap, with the velcro showing through it.

The spare tips stand base-down, so you can see at a glance which is which. A
small groove and a funnel line up the hex key as you close the case.

**Fits**

- Pinecil V2 with the short tips (up to 92 mm)
- For swapping tips on the road: replace one of the two tip screws with an
  M2×5 thumb screw (what the case is shaped around) or an M2×5 hex screw
  (the 1.5 mm hex key travels along)

**Print**

- 4 parts, all PETG, no supports, fits a 180×180 bed
- Print every part standing up: shells on their end cap (the logo is the
  first layer), inserts on their flat outer end
- Settings that worked: dried PETG, 240 °C, 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 layers, seam
  painted or random

**You also need**

- 6 neodymium magnets, Ø3×2 mm (3 per half, mind the polarity)
- Double-sided velcro, 20 mm wide, about 57 cm
- Glue to fix each insert into its shell

**Assembly**

Glue in the magnets, glue each insert into its shell up to the end stops,
let it cure, then thread the velcro: in at the top slot, behind the end cap,
out at the bottom, with the ends overlapping underneath.

Fully parametric (build123d). Source, design notes and test-print slices for
tuning the fit: https://github.com/RoaldO/pinecil-travel-case
